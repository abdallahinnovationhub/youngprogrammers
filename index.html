How the Mudharasah works

  • Select your preferred recitation system.
  • Choose a Surah and the verses you want to practise.
  • Choose 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 verses per turn.
  • In alternating mode, the two people take turns after each selected group of verses.
  • Read the displayed verses aloud.
  • The system compares recognised words with the Qur'an text.
  • Finish your session to see your achievement.

Speech recognition is only an aid. It cannot reliably assess Tajweed, makharij or every Arabic pronunciation. Ask a qualified Qur'an teacher to verify difficult corrections.

🎓 Islamic Learning

The Five Pillars of Islam

  1. Shahadah – Declaration of faith.
  2. Salah – Daily prayers.
  3. Zakah – Obligatory charity.
  4. Sawm – Fasting during Ramadan.
  5. Hajj – Pilgrimage to Makkah for those able.

The Six Articles of Faith

  1. Belief in Allah.
  2. Belief in His angels.
  3. Belief in His revealed books.
  4. Belief in His messengers.
  5. Belief in the Last Day.
  6. Belief in divine decree.

Basic Islamic Knowledge

Qur'an: The holy book revealed to Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

Salah: The five daily prayers.

Ramadan: The ninth month of the Islamic calendar.

Wudu: Ritual washing before prayer.

Ka'bah: The sacred structure in Makkah.
