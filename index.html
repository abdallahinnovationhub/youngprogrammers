🤲 Du'a Collection

Remember Allah in your daily life.

Daily Supplications

1. Du'a Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Transliteration: Bismillah.

Meaning: In the name of Allah.

2. Du'a After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ

Meaning: Praise be to Allah who fed me this and provided it for me without any power or strength from me.

3. Du'a for Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Transliteration: Rabbi zidni ilma.

Meaning: My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

4. Du'a for Forgiveness

رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا وَإِنْ لَمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ

Meaning: Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers.

5. Du'a Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

Meaning: In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.
