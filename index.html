🎓 Islamic Learning Centre

The Five Pillars of Islam

  1. Shahadah – Declaration of faith.
  2. Salah – Performing the five daily prayers.
  3. Zakah – Giving obligatory charity.
  4. Sawm – Fasting during Ramadan.
  5. Hajj – Pilgrimage to Makkah for those able to perform it.

The Six Articles of Faith

  1. Belief in Allah.
  2. Belief in His angels.
  3. Belief in His revealed books.
  4. Belief in His messengers.
  5. Belief in the Last Day.
  6. Belief in divine decree, its good and its difficult aspects.

Basic Islamic Knowledge

Qur'an: The holy book revealed to Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

Salah: The daily prayer performed by Muslims.

Ramadan: The ninth month of the Islamic calendar.

Ka'bah: The sacred structure in Makkah towards which Muslims face in prayer.

Wudu: The ritual washing performed before Salah.
